# Why v1 was not enough — and what v2 fixes

## The problem with Studio v1

Studio v1 proved the plumbing: CRM context, privacy filtering, Ollama, CMA math, content producers and compliance preflight. But its primary UX still behaved like an AI catalog: pick a category, pick a tool, fill a form, generate.

That is not enough to justify using a new workspace every day. A generic AI catalog competes with ChatGPT and eventually loses because the user has to decide which tool to use and manually move between the CRM and the AI workspace.

## The rule for v2

**A feature earns prime UI only if it completes a real job that helps create or serve business.**

The five prime workspaces are:

1. **Command Center** — Decide who deserves attention now and why.
2. **Relationship Copilot** — Understand one person and prepare the next conversation without reconstructing history.
3. **Seller Desk** — Move one seller from lead → property understanding → CMA → appointment prep → launch/updates, with a local net-sheet calculator and compliance gates.
4. **CMA Workbench** — Evidence-first pricing research and math; AI explains but does not invent value.
5. **Content Factory** — One sourced/local idea → video/blog/social campaign → conversation opportunity.

The 36 specialized tools remain available as a secondary toolbox, not the product's primary navigation.

## Why this is a better competitive baseline

- Follow Up Boss's current Smart Messages are generated from a lead profile's texts, calls, emails, notes, activity and timeline. The lesson is **context in the record**, not a separate blank prompt.
- Saleswise's CMA flow emphasizes active/sold comps, interactive refinement and client-ready pricing work. The lesson is **complete task workflows**, not just writing.
- Lofty's current AI Assistant markets the morning experience as priority list → follow-ups → call notes → showing prep and can act across CRM areas. The lesson is **workday orchestration**.

## What v2 intentionally does not fake

- No automatic Zillow/Redfin scraping.
- No MLS access until the authorized data path exists.
- No autonomous texting/emailing/publishing.
- No automatic legal/compliance certification.
- No appraisal claim.
- No preset or assumed commission/compensation rate.
- No core `app.js` rewrite.

## Merge standard

Do not merge because it looks cool. Merge only if the preview passes these questions:

- Does Today tell me who to work without me thinking about filters?
- Does a contact feel more useful because the Copilot already knows the relationship?
- Does Seller Desk reduce tab/app switching?
- Can I build a defensible research CMA without an LLM inventing a number?
- Can I take one local story and turn it into content without rebuilding context four times?
- If Ollama is off, does the CRM still work?

If the answer to those is not yes, v2 is not worth merging.
